# Third-party notices

The buyer package includes a CTranslate2 conversion of [NavAI Whisper Small Uzbek](https://huggingface.co/navai-uz/whisper-small-uzbek). Its model weights are Apache-2.0 licensed. The model directory includes the upstream `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files; `APACHE-2.0.txt` contains the full license text. NavAI documents its training-data attribution requirements on the model card, including the ISSAI/TUIT Uzbek Speech Corpus attribution.

The installer obtains Python dependencies through pip. Their license texts are available in the installed packages' metadata. Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects are separate products and are not included. The Adobe WAV export preset is read from the customer's Adobe installation and is not redistributed.

Optional GigaAM, Rubai corrector, FastConformer, Whisper large and speaker models are not included in this package. Review each model's license and provenance before redistributing it.
